And so we begin!!

Welcome to the site, and the first big comic to be uploaded in full, Night of the Lust Moon!

A little about the comic, I started this off last year for Halloween, and it took me nearly a year to finish it. Life got in the way real hard and I ended up having to move my focus elsewhere for a loooong time, but I didn't want to just up and abandon this one! I really loved what I had going on! I love a good horror movie, and I wanted to do some sort of homage to Werewolf flicks, and there was no better place to do it than Happy Trails! Happy Trails and its odd supernatural energy make a really good excuse for the moon working this way, and only this way, here in the park!

I ended up finishing this thing in full earlier this year around Mayish(?I think? Don't @ me) but it felt kinda silly posting it then such a long time after I started it. So I brought it back now! You can check out the whole thing now if you're a $5 member on my Patreon, or you can stick around and watch the story unfold in real time! I'll be posting an update every day up until Halloween.
